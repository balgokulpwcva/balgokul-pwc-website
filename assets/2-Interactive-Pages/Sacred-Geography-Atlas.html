Sacred Geography Atlas

Temples, pilgrimage circuits, sacred rivers, and the Kumbh and other great melas of India and its neighbors. Drag and zoom the map, follow a yatra, or test yourself.

India is shown with its official boundary, including all of Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh and Aksai Chin; dotted lines mark the Line of Control and Line of Actual Control. Festival months are approximate and follow the lunar calendar, so check a panchang or the organizers for exact dates. Building dates are approximate. Kumbh dates for 2027 and 2028 are from announcements by the Maharashtra and Uttarakhand governments; later years are expected by the 6- and 12-year cycle. Map data: DataMeet (India boundary), Natural Earth (countries and rivers).
